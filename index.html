How to use Good Pet

🐾 Managing Pets: Tap '+' to add a pet. Click on their name chips to switch between profiles.

✨ Quick Praise: Tap the big card or 'Random Praise' to hear a compliment. 'Surprise Me!' grabs new phrases automatically.

🎙️ Voices: Choose from any browser voice installed on your phone or laptop and tweak the pitch or speed.

🔒 Privacy: All pet names, custom compliments, and background photos stay 100% saved in your own browser's local storage.
