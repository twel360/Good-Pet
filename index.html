How to use Good Pet

🐾 Managing Pets: Tap '+' to add a pet. Click on their name chips to switch between profiles.

✨ Quick Praise: Tap the big card or 'Random Praise' to hear a compliment. 'Surprise Me!' grabs new phrases automatically.

🎙️ Voices: Online voices stream free, natural character voices without keys. Device voices use your phone/laptop speech synthesizer.

🔒 Privacy: All pet profiles, photos, and saved praise phrases stay 100% saved in your browser's local storage.
